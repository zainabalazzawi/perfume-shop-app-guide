Who uses it

Two sides of the shop

Customers

Browse the catalog, filter by scent and season, save favorites, fill a basket, and place an order with a delivery pin on the map.

  • • Guests can look around and build a basket
  • • Sign in with Google when ready to buy
  • • Track the order until it is delivered

Shop owner (admin)

A separate admin area on the phone to manage orders, update the catalog, adjust stock and prices, and control what customers see.

  • • Get a Telegram message for every new order
  • • Advance status: preparing → on the way → delivered
  • • See the saved delivery pin; optionally call or WhatsApp the customer if needed

Getting around

How to navigate

Five tabs at the bottom of the screen — always visible on the phone.

Home

Categories, filters, perfume grid, featured reviews, and contact.

Favorites

Perfumes you saved. Works for guests; syncs when you sign in.

Basket

Your cart with a badge showing how many items. Start checkout here.

My Orders

Active orders with a progress tracker, plus past order history.

Account

Name, phone, default delivery location. Sign in or out. Delete account.

On every screen

Bottom tab bar

Switch between Home, Favorites, Basket, My Orders, and Account with one tap. If you are not signed in and open Orders or Account, the app asks you to sign in with Google — then brings you back.

  • 1 Basket shows a count badge when you have items
  • 2 Guests keep favorites and basket on the phone until login
  • 3 After Google sign-in, guest basket merges with your account

Find what you like

  1. 1

    Filters under the header

    Gender, season, scent family, concentration, and brand. You can combine them. Active filters show as chips you can remove.

  2. 2

    Category tiles

    Curated collections from the shop (e.g. gifts, oud, summer). Tap to browse that set.

  3. 3

    Two-column perfume grid

    Each card shows image, name, “from” price for sample and full size, rating (if any), favorite, and basket. Scroll and load more.

On a perfume page

  • Photo gallery and full details
  • Choose Sample or Full, then pick a size
  • Sold-out sizes are greyed out and cannot be selected
  • Sticky bar at the bottom: price, favorite, and Add
  • Add stays disabled until type and size are chosen
  • Customer reviews appear below

Quick add from a card

Tapping the basket icon on a card opens a bottom sheet to pick sample/full and size — same rules as the perfume page.

Out of stock

Fully sold-out perfumes show a badge, grey image, and disabled basket. You can still favorite them. They appear last in the list.

Favorites

Heart icon on cards and the perfume page. No success toast — it just toggles. Find them all under the Favorites tab.

Reviews on Home

Featured reviews appear near the bottom of Home. Contact section is shown for now as information only.

Buttons on each order — what they do

The customer’s delivery location is already saved — on their Account page and again on the order at checkout. The owner does not need WhatsApp or a call to get the address. Call and WhatsApp are only for talking to the customer when needed (optional).

Call · optional

Phone the customer

Only when you need to talk — e.g. they don’t answer the door, or you need a quick clarification. Not used to collect the address.

WhatsApp · optional

Chat the customer when needed

Same idea as Call — a quick chat if something comes up during delivery. Location is already on the order; WhatsApp is not for asking where they live.

Open map

See the saved delivery pin

Shows the location the customer already set (Account and/or checkout). Use this to navigate to them.

Send location

Share that pin with someone

If another person will deliver, the owner can share the already-saved pin. There is no delivery-person login in the app.
